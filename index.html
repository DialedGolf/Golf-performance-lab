About This Project

This project explores whether a golfer's own launch-monitor data can reveal patterns associated with accurate and consistent shots.

Research question

Can a golfer's personal launch-monitor data identify characteristics associated with their most successful shots?

How success is measured

When a target carry is entered, shots can be compared by their distance error and absolute offline. These are measures of outcome, not proof that a particular swing variable caused the result.

Important limitations

A small dataset may not represent future shots. Wind, lie, ball type, club, and other conditions can affect results. Analyze each club separately and collect shots under reasonably consistent conditions.
